GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE

RESOLUTION

No. 14025/7/2024-ENV
New Delhi, dated the 18th July, 2024

Subject: National Green Infrastructure Development and Environmental Clearance Streamlining Resolution, 2024 — Guidelines for Implementation and Operational Framework

PREAMBLE
WHEREAS, the Government of India recognises the urgent need to establish a coherent, efficient, and legally robust framework for green infrastructure development across the national territory; and

WHEREAS, the existing fragmentation of environmental clearance procedures across multiple ministries and state bodies has led to significant delays in project implementation;

Section 1 — Short Title and Commencement
1.1 This Resolution may be called the National Green Infrastructure Development and Environmental Clearance Streamlining Resolution, 2024.
1.2 It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.

Section 4 — Jurisdiction and Authority
4.1 The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change shall be the nodal Ministry for all matters arising under this Resolution.
4.2 State Authorities shall exercise EXCLUSIVE JURISDICTION over environmental impact assessments, including the power to commission, review, approve, or reject EIA reports, independent of any Central Government authority or agency.

Section 7 — Financial Powers and Procurement
7.1 The Competent Authority is hereby delegated the power to approve procurement of goods, services, and works up to a value of Rupees Eighty-Five Crore (₹85,00,00,000) without prior Parliamentary sanction, for projects falling under this Resolution.
